How BorrowLend works

Connecting book owners and readers locally in 3 effortless steps

01

Discover a book

Browse hundreds of pre-loved books nearby. Filter by genre, condition, or check whether it's available for weekly rent or outright buy.

02

Request to rent or buy

Send a quick request to the owner. Message them securely regarding pickup time, book condition, or duration.

03

Arrange local pickup

Meet safely in your neighborhood (cafe, apartment lobby, metro). Pay cash or UPI directly upon receiving the book.
